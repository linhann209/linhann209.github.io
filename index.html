Featured prototype

Engineering and Maintenance Knowledge Assistant

A document-first exploration of a recurring industrial problem: the right information often exists, but it is scattered across manuals, safety procedures, technical guidance, and maintenance history.

01

The problem

Different terminology, exact equipment identifiers, document revisions, and disconnected sources make technical information slow to find and difficult to apply consistently.

02

The workflow

The assistant retrieves relevant evidence, presents safety prerequisites and explicit inspection steps, identifies missing observations, and revises guidance as new facts are supplied.

03

The boundary

The public prototype uses synthetic sources and a transparent local simulation. It demonstrates interaction and control design—not production engineering accuracy or live LLM performance.

Production direction

Designed as an intelligence layer—not another document repository.

The intended enterprise architecture complements existing knowledge, document-management, and engineering-information systems.

  1. 01Governed sources

    Authorized documents, metadata, revisions, equipment relationships, and permissions remain in their systems of record.

  2. 02Hybrid retrieval

    Exact search protects tags and fault codes; embedding-based retrieval connects related meaning expressed in different language.

  3. 03Agentic reasoning

    An LLM interprets selected evidence, asks focused follow-up questions, and structures a response with source citations.

  4. 04Human review

    Deterministic controls enforce boundaries while qualified professionals remain responsible for decisions and governed actions.
